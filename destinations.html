30 destinations. Three places beyond the map.

These are miniature interpretations of real places, followed by three imaginary realms. Four stops use bridges; 29 use variations of 12 other puzzle families. The rules below describe how to play without giving away every solution.

How a journey works

  1. Choose an animal and a destination. Walk with the on-screen joystick; approach discoveries or use the action button shown.
  2. Complete the three local discoveries and the destination’s star-spirit puzzle. Visible goals explain the task; tap the light bulb only if you want a hint.
  3. Return to the stamp platform by the landmark. Collect the passport stamp and choose your next stop.
