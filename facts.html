Pawprint Atlas: official facts & support

Pawprint Atlas is an independent 3D travel puzzle game for iPhone by Yuanlin Deng. Explore miniature destinations with an animal companion, solve puzzles and collect passport stamps at your own pace.

What makes the puzzles different?

Guilin connects waterways, Paris balances weights, London follows a visible musical score, and Antarctica uses sliding movement on ice. Some places share a core rule with different scenery and configurations. The game does not claim 33 unrelated puzzle mechanics.
