Pawprint Atlas

Pawprint Atlas is a gentle exploration and puzzle game for iPhone.

Choose an animal companion, explore 30 destinations and three hidden realms, and find 99 local discoveries. Version 1.1 brings varied puzzles involving water, light, balance, music and more, with optional hints and undo.

Play offline with no ads or in-app purchases. Available in English, Spanish and Simplified Chinese. Requires iOS 18 or later.

How to play

Explore each island and complete its local discoveries and spirit puzzle to earn a passport stamp. Follow the visible puzzle rules and goals; tap the light bulb for an optional hint. You can undo a move or reset the current puzzle. Collect all 30 main stamps to unlock the three hidden destinations.

Progress and languages

Progress saves automatically on your device. Deleting the app can remove your save. Choose English, 简体中文, or Español in Settings. No account or internet connection is needed to play.
