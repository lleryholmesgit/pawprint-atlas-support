A LITTLE PAW. A BIG WORLD.

Pawprint Atlas

A gentle 3D journey through miniature worlds.

Choose one of eight animal companions, collect postcards, and solve folding star paths across 30 destinations. Earn all 30 main destination stamps to discover three hidden worlds.

Support

Need a hand? Email ydengeh@yahoo.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, destination, and what happened. Please do not send passwords or payment information.

How to play

Use the on-screen joystick to walk. Gather three postcards, enter the Folding Star Path, and bring the star spirit home. Return to the green stamp platform by the landmark to stamp your passport.

Progress and languages

Progress saves automatically on your device. Deleting the app can remove your save. Choose English, 简体中文, or Español in Settings. No account or internet connection is needed to play.

Purchase help
