Before your first trip

Can I play Pawprint Atlas offline?

Yes. After downloading the game, exploration, puzzles and progress saving work without an internet connection. No account is required.

Is it free, or is there a subscription?

Pawprint Atlas is a paid download: $0.49 on the US App Store. There are no ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases. Check the App Store for current availability and price.

Which devices and languages are supported?

Pawprint Atlas is for iPhone running iOS 18 or later. The game supports English, Simplified Chinese and Spanish. The current release is available on the US App Store.

Does the game have a timer?

No. Puzzle rules and goals are visible, and specific hints appear only when you tap the light-bulb button. You can undo moves or reset a puzzle.

How many levels and puzzle types are there?

There are 30 main destinations and 3 hidden realms. Four destinations have bridge puzzles; the other 29 use variations of 12 non-bridge puzzle families. There are 99 local discoveries in total.

How do I unlock the hidden destinations?

Collect all 30 main passport stamps to unlock the three hidden realms. Complete each destination’s local discoveries and spirit puzzle, then return to its stamp platform.

Does Pawprint Atlas collect personal data?

The game has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking SDKs. Progress and language preferences are stored on your device. See the privacy policy for details about support emails and website hosting.

Where is my progress saved?

Progress saves automatically on your device. Deleting the app can remove its local save. Device or iCloud backups may retain a copy; the developer cannot access your save.
