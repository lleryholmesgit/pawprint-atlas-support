Privacy Policy

Updated September 20, 2026

Pawprint Atlas is published by Yuanlin Deng. The game does not collect, transmit, or share personal data from gameplay. No account is required. There are no ads, analytics, tracking technologies, or third-party data collection SDKs. The app does not request location, camera, microphone, photos, or contacts access.

Local storage and backups

Progress and language preferences are saved on your device. We cannot access them. Deleting the app removes its local save. Your device or iCloud backups may retain a copy, and restoring a backup may restore progress. You control backups through your Apple settings.

Support correspondence

If you voluntarily email ydengeh@yahoo.com, we receive your email address and the information you provide solely to answer your request. Email is processed by our email provider. Do not send passwords or payment details. Request deletion of support correspondence at the same address; records required by law may be retained.

Purchases

Apple processes the App Store purchase. We do not receive your payment card information. For refunds, use Apple's Report a Problem service.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from players, including children. It has no chat, user-generated content, ads, or in-app purchases.

This website

These pages use no analytics, cookies, or contact forms added by us. They are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information such as IP addresses to operate and secure the hosting service under its own privacy statement.

Changes and contact

Policy updates will be published on this page. Questions or data requests: ydengeh@yahoo.com.
